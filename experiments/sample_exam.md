$$A = \begin{bmatrix} 3 & -5 & 2 \\ 7 & -12 & 3 \\ 1 & -2 & -1 \end{bmatrix}, \quad B = \begin{bmatrix} -2 & 3 & 1 \\ 0 & 3+c & 9-c^2 \\ -2 & 3 & -8+c^2 \end{bmatrix}$$

に対して、次の線形写像を考える.

$$egin{align} f:\mathbb{R}^3 &
ightarrow \mathbb{R}^3, & f(oldsymbol{x}) = Aoldsymbol{x} & (oldsymbol{x} \in \mathbb{R}^3) \ g:\mathbb{R}^3 &
ightarrow \mathbb{R}^3, & g(oldsymbol{x}) = Boldsymbol{x} & (oldsymbol{x} \in \mathbb{R}^3) \ \end{cases}$$
